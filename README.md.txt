🔹 Project Overview
Analyzed an HR dataset to study employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and compensation trends. The objective was to identify key attrition drivers and generate data-driven insights to support HR decision-making.

🔹 Dataset Details
Records: 1,400+ employees
Data Type: Structured HR dataset
Key Attributes:
Employee demographics (Age, Gender, Education, Marital Status)
Job details (Department, Job Role, Job Level, Salary Slab)
Attrition and satisfaction metrics
Experience, tenure, and performance indicators

🔹 Tools & Techniques Used
Advanced Excel: Data cleaning, transformation, and analysis
Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
Dashboards & Slicers
Business Analytics & KPI Analysis

🔹 Business Questions Answered
What is the overall employee attrition rate?
Which departments and job roles experience the highest attrition?
How does attrition vary by age group and gender?
What is the relationship between salary slabs and attrition?
How do job satisfaction, work-life balance, and tenure impact attrition?

🔹 Key KPIs Tracked
Total Employees
Attrition Count and Attrition Rate
Average Age
Average Salary
Average Years at Company

🔹 Key Insights
Overall attrition rate is approximately 16%, with higher attrition in specific job roles.
Employees in the 26–35 age group show the highest attrition levels.
Lower salary slabs contribute disproportionately to employee attrition.
Certain departments and roles require targeted retention strategies.

🔹 Business Impact
Enabled quick identification of high-risk attrition segments.
Reduced manual analysis time by ~60% through automated dashboards.

Delivered clear, actionable insights to support retention and compensation planning.
